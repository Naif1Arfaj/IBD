01 / PURPOSE

Intended clinical use

Designed to support nurses in organising patient information and reviewing estimated IBD risk with the clinical team.

  • Review recorded patient information.
  • Understand the estimate and the factors influencing it.
  • Consult sources and prepare a printable assessment.

Current scope: demonstration only. It is not a diagnostic or treatment tool.

03 / PROFESSIONAL REVIEW

Clinical safety & human oversight

What a result means

  • A risk estimate is not a diagnosis.
  • A low estimate does not exclude IBD or override symptoms and red flags.
  • Contributing factors explain the calculation; they do not establish causation.

The human remains in control

  • The nurse checks the recorded information and reviews the findings.
  • The clinical team judges the relevance of literature and follow-up guidance.
  • No diagnosis, referral or treatment decision is made automatically.
Demonstration results must not guide patient care.Estimates and explanations must not be used for diagnosis or treatment.
